**New**

- **Attach at login.** Check **Attach at login** in a target's settings and it attaches by itself when you log in. The app opens without a window. If a target can't be reached yet, it keeps trying for 5 minutes. Anything still unattached then gets an orange warning on its row and on the menu bar icon, and the window opens. If everything attaches, you see nothing.
- Checking the box adds the app to **Open at Login**, which works only when the app is in /Applications. If a target is marked but the app won't open at login, Targets shows a banner and a button that fixes it.
- On a login launch, update checks wait until the targets have finished attaching.

**Fixes**

- After you log out with a volume attached, the next attach mounts it again. It used to find the leftover disk device, report the target attached, and mount nothing.
- A target that doesn't answer now reports "The target did not answer in time." with what to check, instead of "iSCSIKit.DeadlineError error 0".
- The filesystem extension now releases a volume that macOS loaded but never mounted, instead of keeping its session open.

**Uninstall**

- Uninstall also removes the app from Open at Login.

**Upgrading**

- After the update, Setup asks you to **Reinstall** the background service. On macOS 26, click **Enable** again as well.
